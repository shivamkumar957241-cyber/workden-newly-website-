Refund Policy

Please read this policy carefully before making any payment.

Welcome to WorkDen.online. This policy outlines important information regarding payments and refund conditions.

1. General Refund Policy

Payments made on WorkDen.online are generally non-refundable once portal access and onboarding services have been activated. Upon successful payment confirmation, access credentials are generated and shared with the user.

Refunds will only be considered in cases of duplicate payment, verified technical failure, or if portal access is not provided within a reasonable time after successful payment.

2. Digital Nature of Services

WorkDen provides digital access to tasks, dashboards, onboarding materials, and platform features. As these services are delivered digitally and access is granted upon payment verification, they cannot typically be revoked once activated.

3. User Responsibility

Users are advised to carefully review all service details, eligibility requirements, and platform guidelines before making payment. By completing a transaction, you confirm that you understand and agree to this Refund Policy.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after activation
  • Inactivity after receiving portal access
  • Lack of understanding of platform guidelines
  • Dissatisfaction not related to technical failure
  • Technical issues arising from the user's device or internet
  • Personal reasons unrelated to service delivery

5. Chargeback & Dispute Policy

In case of any payment dispute or chargeback, users are encouraged to contact support first for resolution. Initiating false or fraudulent disputes may result in account suspension and further review as per applicable policies.

6. Agreement Confirmation

By making payment on WorkDen.online, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Refund Policy.
